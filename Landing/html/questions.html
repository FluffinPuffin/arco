General

To login, click on the "Settings" tab in the header, or use the login option from the home screen. Enter your email and password to access your account.

To change your payment method, go to Settings and navigate to the Grown-Ups section. Click on "Payment" and select "Edit" to update your payment information.

To change your username, first click on the 'Settings' tab located inside the header. Next, navigate to the 'Profile' section and click on the 'Edit' button that corresponds with 'Name'.

Privacy

We use industry-standard encryption to protect your personal data. Your information is never shared with third parties without your consent.

Send us an email at support@arco.com with your account details and request for account deletion.

Troubleshooting

Ensure you have an internet connection and try refreshing the page. If the issue persists, try clearing your browser cache or contact support.

Grant camera permissions when prompted. Ensure adequate lighting and hold the QR code steady in front of the camera. If it still fails, try a different browser.

Accessibility

Yes, we strive to ensure our app works well with major screen readers. If you encounter issues, please contact us so we can improve.

You can adjust text size through your device or browser settings. We are working on built-in accessibility controls for future updates.

Cookie Policy

(as of February 5, 2026)

Arco uses cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience on our platform. This policy explains what cookies are and how we use them.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and understand how you interact with content.

Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies: These are necessary for the platform to function properly. They enable core features like user authentication, session management, and security. You cannot opt out of these cookies.

Functional Cookies: These remember your preferences, such as language settings, display options, and game configurations, to provide a personalized experience.

Analytics Cookies: These help us understand how users interact with our platform, which pages are visited most, and where users encounter issues. This data helps us improve our services.

Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can choose to block or delete cookies, but this may affect your ability to use certain features of our platform.
